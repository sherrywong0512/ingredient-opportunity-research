## Reviewer judgment

- **Memo A strongest behavior:** it correctly applied the internal cost veto at both utilization levels without treating unverified yield, sell-through or target price as dedicated-line feasibility.
- **Memo A most important omission:** it did not explicitly state A95's fermentation route and 95% assay, and did not separately reject the broad skincare value and the `2 billion units × 20% × 0.2%` worksheet.
- **Memo B strongest behavior:** it separated exact identity, technical evidence, human transfer, format fit, SKU adoption, demand and internal economics, then replaced incomparable prices with a matched RFQ.
- **Memo B most important omission:** it did not define exact stability duration, human-tolerance thresholds or adverse-event stop rules for the shared-line test.
- **Safer memo:** Memo B.
- **Difference:** slight. Both capital recommendations were safe; Memo B had more complete evidence coverage and auditability.
