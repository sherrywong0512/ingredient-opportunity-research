# Three-case Direct vs Skill comparison

## Executive result

Three frozen synthetic capital-decision cases compared the same model family working directly from an evidence pack versus working with `ingredient-opportunity-research`.

| Case | Direct | Skill | Delta | Direct hard failure | Skill hard failure | Blind-review difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FermaDHA-X expansion | 96 | 100 | +4 | 0 | 0 | Small measurable improvement |
| MycoPro-PV9 pet-food scale-up | 92 | 99 | +7 | 0 | 0 | Substantive |
| DermaBis-A95 skincare line | 95 | 99 | +4 | 0 | 0 | Slight |
| **Total / 300** | **283** | **298** | **+15** | **0** | **0** | — |
| **Mean / 100** | **94.3** | **99.3** | **+5.0** | **0** | **0** | — |

The Direct group was already strong and made the same safe headline decision in every case. The Skill did not reduce hard failures in this sample because neither group had one. Its observed advantage was narrower and more credible: more complete capital-decision audit coverage, more explicit treatment of incomparable evidence, and more operational next-evidence gates.

## Where the Skill changed behavior

| Case | Additional behavior observed in the Skill output |
|---|---|
| FermaDHA-X | Named the price-basis mismatches and tied expansion to matched qualified demand, allocable saleable supply and contribution economics |
| MycoPro-PV9 | Explicitly disposed of the broad retail-market figure, separated nameplate from qualified saleable output and exposed ramp/working-capital unknowns |
| DermaBis-A95 | Preserved route and assay identity, rejected the assumption-driven unit worksheet and made the matched RFQ the economic evidence gate |

## Portfolio interpretation

Supported claim:

> In three preregistered synthetic decision cases, the Skill condition scored 298/300 versus 283/300 for the same-model Direct condition, a descriptive mean difference of +5.0 points. Both groups avoided all hard failures; the Skill's advantage was audit completeness and actionability rather than a different headline decision.

Unsupported claims:

- the Skill is generally 5 points better across ingredients, models or users;
- the Skill improves factual retrieval accuracy or predicts real markets more accurately;
- the Skill saves 80%–90% expert time;
- the Skill improves sales, project approval, capacity utilization or investment return;
- the three fixtures are an unbiased benchmark.

## Product decision

Retain the current Skill. Do not add a new rule solely to chase the remaining one-point omissions:

- The MycoPro omission was a finite summary omission; the Skill already requires adverse/null evidence.
- The DermaBis omission was missing test duration and thresholds in a 1,200-character memo; the Skill already requires measurable time points and success/failure rules.

The next valid test should use real frozen evidence packs, human domain experts, repeated runs, recorded model versions and measured completion/edit time. This is more decision-relevant than overfitting the Skill to these synthetic rubrics.
