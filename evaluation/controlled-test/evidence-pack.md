# Frozen evidence pack: FermaDHA-X capacity decision

## Decision request

The supplier is considering whether to add a new fermentation line for `FermaDHA-X` for adult omega-3 gummies in China. Prepare a Chinese decision memo of no more than 900 Chinese characters that:

1. estimates the supply-demand gap if the evidence permits;
2. advises `expand`, `run a bounded validation`, or `do not expand`;
3. separates verified facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns;
4. names the smallest next evidence needed to change the decision.

Use only this frozen evidence pack. Do not browse or add facts.

## Evidence available at the cutoff

- Product identity: `FermaDHA-X` is a fermentation-derived oil. The proposed China gummy grade is intended to contain 40% DHA. The current plant also makes a 30% DHA oil and other fermentation products on a shared line.
- Plant disclosure for 2025: nameplate capacity was 2,000 tonnes of mixed fermentation oils; actual mixed-oil production was 900 tonnes; mixed-oil sales were 720 tonnes; year-end mixed-oil inventory was 260 tonnes. No allocation by grade or country was disclosed.
- Supplier planning assumption: the new line would add 1,000 tonnes per year of nameplate capacity. Qualification yield, utilization, saleable output, inventory policy and opportunity cost versus other products are unknown.
- Nutrition association estimate: Chinese adults consume materially less omega-3 than a recommended-intake benchmark. The association converts the population intake difference into 40,000 tonnes of oil-equivalent annual need. It contains no ingredient purchase orders, accepted prices or gummy adoption assumptions.
- Market-report forecast: global aquafeed could face a 10,000-tonne fish-oil deficit in 2030. The report does not isolate China, adult nutrition, gummies, fermentation-derived oil or 40% DHA grade.
- Commercial log: six non-binding buyer inquiries mention a combined 2,600 tonnes. Two inquiries are from the same buying group; requested DHA concentrations range from 20% to 40%; delivery windows range from 2026 to 2028; three omit target price and Incoterms; none records technical qualification or a binding order.
- Current downstream evidence: two China gummy brands list algal DHA, but the accessible labels do not identify supplier, production route, DHA concentration or annual use amount.
- Price evidence: one 20% DHA retail listing, one 40% DHA distributor indication and one supplier target price are available. Quantities, tax, freight, payment terms and dates are not aligned.
- Regulation and technical fit: this pack contains no exact-product China authorization, formulation stability, sensory, shelf-life or customer qualification evidence for `FermaDHA-X`.

## Constraints

- The capital decision must be made in six weeks.
- Management may fund RFQs, dossier review and pilot qualification before committing to the line.
- Do not treat nameplate capacity as saleable supply or recommended intake as purchase demand.
