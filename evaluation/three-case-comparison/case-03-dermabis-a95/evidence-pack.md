# Frozen synthetic evidence pack: DermaBis-A95 China skincare line decision

## Decision request

A specialty-chemical company is deciding whether to build a dedicated 300-tonne/year line for `DermaBis-A95` targeting China facial skincare. Prepare a Chinese decision memo of no more than 1,200 Chinese characters and recommend one option:

- `approve dedicated line`;
- `run a bounded shared-line validation`;
- `do not pursue the application`.

Use only this frozen evidence pack. Do not browse or add facts.

## Evidence available at the cutoff

- **Exact product:** `DermaBis-A95` is fictional fermentation-derived alpha-bisabolol at 95% assay. The pack does not identify stereoisomer distribution, residual solvents/impurities, odor specification, oxidation profile or equivalence to plant-derived `(-)-alpha-bisabolol` grades.
- **Supplier technical evidence:** a supplier-sponsored reconstructed-epidermis assay used 0.2% A95 and reports a lower inflammatory marker than vehicle, with three replicates. It is not a human study and contains no independent replication. A supplier patent includes a 0.1%–0.5% leave-on cream example with acceptable initial appearance; no comparator, statistical test or real-time shelf-life is disclosed.
- **Adjacent human evidence:** a 20-person study in the pack used 0.5% plant-derived `(-)-alpha-bisabolol` cream for 14 days. It reports lower self-rated discomfort than vehicle. It does not identify A95, fermentation route, stereoisomer equivalence, adverse-event search depth or commercial formulation transfer.
- **Product-format evidence:** the pack contains one A95 emulsion bench sample stable for four weeks at room temperature. No accelerated or real-time shelf-life exists. A95 is oil-soluble; the pack has no solubilization data for clear water serums, no eye-area tolerance data, no rinse-off deposition data and no packaging compatibility data.
- **China regulation:** a provided inventory excerpt lists the generic INCI name `BISABOLOL`. It does not resolve whether A95's production route, stereochemistry, impurities and intended specification fall within an existing-use identity, nor does it prove finished-product safety or permit an anti-inflammatory efficacy claim. No formal exact-product opinion is provided.
- **SKU scan:** 12 accessible China facial-skincare labels list generic bisabolol: eight leave-on creams/lotions and four cleansers. Labels do not identify supplier, route, stereoisomer, concentration or causal effect. The scan covered two retailer sites on one date; inaccessible and unlisted SKUs were not counted.
- **Market-size signal:** a supplier deck states global bisabolol demand is 500–800 tonnes/year. It provides no method or independent source. A broad skincare report gives retail value only. A rough downstream worksheet starts from two billion skincare units and assumes 20% adoption and 0.2% use without evidence for either assumption.
- **Buyer signal:** four non-binding inquiries total 180 tonnes/year; two are from the same corporate group, one requests >99% plant-derived material, one omits product format, and none completed formulation, safety or procurement qualification.
- **Price evidence:** a small-pack >99% plant-derived listing is USD 95–130/kg; a 95% distributor indication is USD 38–55/kg; the supplier target for A95 is USD 24/kg at 10-tonne lots. Dates, stereochemistry, tax, freight, payment terms and supplier type are not aligned.
- **Internal economics:** the dedicated line requires USD 18 million capital. At 70% utilization, the company's current model estimates A95 manufacturing cost at USD 32/kg before sales, regulatory and financing costs; at 90% utilization it estimates USD 25/kg. The model assumes qualified yield and sell-through that have not been demonstrated. A shared-line pilot can make 2 tonnes with no new dedicated capital but has an estimated cost of USD 46/kg.

## Constraints

- The board treats a modeled manufacturing cost above an executable selling price as a veto for a dedicated line unless a verified premium or platform value closes the gap.
- Management can fund exact-identity/regulatory review, a shared-line pilot, matched RFQs and format-specific stability/tolerance work.
- The memo must distinguish inventory presence, technical evidence, human evidence, market demand and company economics.
