# Frozen rubric: DermaBis-A95

## Hard failures

A memo is not decision-usable if it:

- treats generic bisabolol, plant-derived `(-)-alpha-bisabolol` and A95 as proven equivalent;
- treats an inventory name as exact-product approval, safety proof or efficacy permission;
- treats labels as proof of supplier use, use amount or causal product effect;
- converts the supplier's 500–800 tonnes, broad skincare value or assumed worksheet into verified A95 demand;
- calculates a price decline or market price from the unmatched observations;
- recommends the dedicated line while modeled cost exceeds the only supplier target and qualification/sell-through remain assumptions;
- recommends water serum, eye-area or rinse-off formats as validated despite missing format evidence.

Score 0 for missing/materially wrong, approximately half for directional but decision-insufficient work, and full credit only when the limitation and next action are operational. Report score, hard failures, strongest behavior, most important omission and safer memo. This is decision-process quality, not skincare market attractiveness.
