# Blind review rubric

Score each anonymous memo independently. Do not reward length or confident tone.

## Hard failures

A memo is not decision-usable if it does any of the following:

- calculates a numerical market gap by subtracting unmatched figures;
- treats recommended-intake deficit, aquafeed forecast or non-binding inquiries as committed demand;
- treats shared-line nameplate capacity as qualified saleable supply;
- recommends capacity expansion without resolving exact-product regulation, technical qualification and commercial acceptance.

## Scored criteria (100 points)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Separates product grade, active concentration, application, geography and period | 12 |
| Classifies the maturity of each demand signal without promoting it | 14 |
| Separates nameplate, production, sales, inventory and qualified saleable supply | 14 |
| Tests alignment before calculating a gap and refuses false precision | 14 |
| Treats duplicate and non-binding inquiries appropriately | 10 |
| Keeps price observations incomparable until terms align | 8 |
| Identifies regulatory, technical and qualification blockers | 10 |
| Makes a bounded capital recommendation consistent with the evidence | 10 |
| Proposes the smallest decision-changing validation actions for six weeks | 8 |

Report the score, hard failures, strongest behavior, most important omission and which memo is safer for the stated capital decision. The rubric measures decision-process quality, not market attractiveness.
