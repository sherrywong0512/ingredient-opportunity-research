## Review conclusion

All 18 responses made the same safe headline recommendation: do not build a dedicated line yet; run bounded validation first. The observed difference is therefore framework coverage and repeatability, not headline correctness. The blind reviewer was from the same model family and was not a chemical-industry expert; this review cannot establish industry recognition or real-market accuracy.
