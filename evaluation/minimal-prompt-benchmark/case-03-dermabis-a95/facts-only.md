# Synthetic facts only: DermaBis-A95

- `DermaBis-A95` is fictional fermentation-derived alpha-bisabolol at 95% assay. The pack does not identify stereoisomer distribution, residual solvents/impurities, odor specification, oxidation profile or equivalence to plant-derived `(-)-alpha-bisabolol` grades.
- A supplier-sponsored reconstructed-epidermis assay used 0.2% A95 and reports a lower inflammatory marker than vehicle, with three replicates. It is not a human study and contains no independent replication.
- A supplier patent includes a 0.1%–0.5% leave-on cream example with acceptable initial appearance; no comparator, statistical test or real-time shelf-life is disclosed.
- A 20-person study used 0.5% plant-derived `(-)-alpha-bisabolol` cream for 14 days and reports lower self-rated discomfort than vehicle. It does not identify A95, fermentation route, stereoisomer equivalence, adverse-event search depth or commercial-formulation transfer.
- One A95 emulsion bench sample was stable for four weeks at room temperature. A95 is oil-soluble. There is no solubilization evidence for clear water serums, eye-area tolerance data, rinse-off deposition data or packaging-compatibility data.
- A provided China inventory excerpt lists generic INCI name `BISABOLOL`. It does not resolve whether A95's route, stereochemistry, impurities and intended specification fall within an existing-use identity, nor prove finished-product safety or permit an anti-inflammatory efficacy claim.
- Twelve accessible China facial-skincare labels list generic bisabolol: eight leave-on creams/lotions and four cleansers. Labels do not identify supplier, route, stereoisomer, concentration or causal effect. The scan covered two retailer sites on one date; inaccessible and unlisted SKUs were not counted.
- A supplier deck states global bisabolol demand is 500–800 tonnes/year without method or independent source. A broad skincare report gives retail value only. A worksheet starts from two billion skincare units and assumes 20% adoption and 0.2% use without evidence for either assumption.
- Four non-binding inquiries total 180 tonnes/year; two are from the same corporate group, one requests >99% plant-derived material, one omits product format, and none completed formulation, safety or procurement qualification.
- A small-pack >99% plant-derived listing is USD 95–130/kg; a 95% distributor indication is USD 38–55/kg; the supplier target for A95 is USD 24/kg at 10-tonne lots. Dates, stereochemistry, tax, freight, payment terms and supplier type are not aligned.
- The dedicated line requires USD 18 million. At 70% utilization, modeled A95 manufacturing cost is USD 32/kg before sales, regulatory and financing costs; at 90% utilization it is USD 25/kg. Qualified yield and sell-through are unverified. A shared-line pilot can make 2 tonnes without new dedicated capital at an estimated USD 46/kg.
- The board treats modeled manufacturing cost above executable selling price as a veto for a dedicated line unless a verified premium or platform value closes the gap. Management can fund exact-identity/regulatory review, a shared-line pilot, matched RFQs and format-specific stability/tolerance work.
