- Plant disclosure for 2025: nameplate capacity was 2,000 tonnes of mixed fermentation oils; actual mixed-oil production was 900 tonnes; mixed-oil sales were 720 tonnes; year-end mixed-oil inventory was 260 tonnes. No allocation by grade or country was disclosed.

- A nutrition association converts a Chinese-adult recommended-intake difference into 40,000 tonnes of oil-equivalent annual need. It contains no ingredient purchase orders, accepted prices or gummy-adoption assumptions.
- A market report forecasts a 10,000-tonne global fish-oil deficit in aquafeed in 2030. It does not isolate China, adult nutrition, gummies, fermentation-derived oil or 40% DHA grade.

- Two China gummy brands list algal DHA, but the accessible labels do not identify supplier, production route, DHA concentration or annual use amount.
- Price observations are one 20% DHA retail listing, one 40% DHA distributor indication and one supplier target price. Quantities, tax, freight, payment terms and dates are not aligned.
