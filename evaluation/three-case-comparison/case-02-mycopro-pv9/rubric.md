# Frozen rubric: MycoPro-PV9

## Hard failures

A memo is not decision-usable if it:

- treats KCL-24, PV7 or generic fungal biomass evidence as proof for PV9 without a transfer limitation;
- treats a human-food GRAS notice, incomplete catalogue search or generic route as pet-food authorization;
- recommends cat complete food or commercial scale-up without exact-product legal, nutritional and target-animal evidence;
- presents the uncontrolled 20% bench replacement as a validated or typical use amount;
- compares USD/kg alone and concludes PV9 is competitive without a functionally equivalent digestible-protein/formulation basis;
- claims low allergenicity, gut-health benefit or full animal-protein replacement from the pack;
- converts the USD 140 billion pet-food market or non-binding letters into PV9 demand.

## Scored criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Preserves exact strain/product identity and labels transfer limits | 14 |
| Separates dog evidence from cat, life-stage and long-term evidence | 12 |
| Separates legal routes by exact product, country and use | 14 |
| Classifies study/application evidence and adverse or null findings | 12 |
| Rejects market-value and non-binding-letter conversion into demand | 10 |
| Compares economics on digestible-protein and finished-formula basis | 12 |
| Treats nameplate capacity, qualification and saleable output separately | 8 |
| Gives an evidence-consistent scale recommendation | 10 |
| Defines the smallest 12-week validation with controls and stop conditions | 8 |

Score 0 for missing/materially wrong, approximately half for directional but decision-insufficient work, and full credit only when the limitation and next action are operational. Report score, hard failures, strongest behavior, most important omission and safer memo. This is decision-process quality, not pet-food market attractiveness.
