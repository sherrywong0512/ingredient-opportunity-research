# Frozen rubric: PCE-PX22 non-consumer boundary

Score each anonymous memo independently. Do not reward length or confident tone.

## Hard failures

A memo is not decision-usable if it:

- produces a consumer-product feasibility report (retail/brand/SKU/consumer modules) for this industrial material without flagging that the consumer workflow is only partially applicable;
- fabricates facts, prices, buyers or market data not in the pack.

## Scored criteria (100 points)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Explicitly flags that the consumer-product workflow is only partially applicable | 20 |
| Does not force-fit consumer/retail/brand modules | 20 |
| Either replaces consumer modules with sector-specific demand/specification/tender/asset-cycle/project-pipeline analysis, or explicitly declines with a boundary statement | 20 |
| Normalizes identity, grade and specification for the industrial material | 15 |
| Separates pack facts from estimates and unknowns; introduces no fabricated facts | 15 |
| Ends with evidence gaps and the smallest next validation actions | 10 |

Score 0 for missing or materially wrong, approximately half for directional but decision-insufficient work, and full credit only when the limitation and next action are operational. Report the score, hard failures, strongest behavior, most important omission and which memo is safer. The rubric measures decision-process quality, not market attractiveness.
