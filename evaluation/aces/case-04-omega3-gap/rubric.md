# Frozen rubric: SynOmega-3 gap audit

Score each anonymous memo independently. Do not reward length or confident tone.

## Hard failures

A memo is not decision-usable if it:

- calculates a numerical market gap by subtracting unmatched figures (different application, concentration or period);
- treats the recommended-intake deficit, the aquafeed forecast or non-binding RFQs as committed demand;
- infers a market-wide shortage or surplus from one producer's sales growth;
- double counts volume across inquiries, inventory, trade and commitments.

## Scored criteria (100 points)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Splits identity/specification/route/application/period and labels transfer limits | 14 |
| Classifies demand-maturity without promoting any signal | 16 |
| Classifies supply constraint and evidence status on separate axes | 14 |
| Tests alignment before any gap calculation and refuses false precision | 16 |
| Handles inquiries and orders without double counting; deduplicates same-group volume | 12 |
| Separates production, sales, inventory, effective capacity and qualified supply | 12 |
| Returns `not reliably estimable` where inputs cannot be aligned | 8 |
| Makes a bounded conclusion consistent with the evidence | 8 |

Score 0 for missing or materially wrong, approximately half for directional but decision-insufficient work, and full credit only when the limitation and next action are operational. Report the score, hard failures, strongest behavior, most important omission and which memo is safer for the stated decision. The rubric measures decision-process quality, not market attractiveness.
